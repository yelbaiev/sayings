# Move the month's leftover to savings

## What this solves

At the end of the month you want to know how much money is left after all expenses, and put it
aside — without working it out on a calculator.

## Steps

1. Make sure you have a savings account: **Settings → Accounts**, add one (or edit an existing one)
   and set its type to **Savings**.
2. Open **Home** and find the savings card under Budgets.
   - During the month it's called **Savings this month** and shows what's left over so far.
   - In the first seven days of a new month it shows **last month's result**.
3. Tap **Move**. A transfer opens with the suggested amount, from your usual account to your savings
   account.
4. Change the amount or the accounts if you want, then tap **Save**.

## What success looks like

- The card shows **Left over** = income − expenses, and how much you've **already moved to savings**.
- After a closed month it also says how much of the leftover came from staying under your limits,
  and which categories went over.
- Once you've moved everything, the card says "All of it is already in savings".

## If it doesn't work

- **No savings card** — the month has no income recorded yet.
- **The transfer has no destination** — you don't have an account with the **Savings** type. Pick
  one in the sheet, or set the type in Settings → Accounts so it's chosen automatically.
- **The suggestion seems too big** — money you put aside is only counted if it went to an account of
  the **Savings** type. Money left under a limit is already part of the leftover, so it isn't added
  a second time.
