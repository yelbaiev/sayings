# See how your budgets are doing

## What this solves

You set a monthly limit for some categories (groceries, eating out) and want to know, at a glance,
whether you're still within it — without digging through Settings.

## Steps

1. Set a limit once: **Settings → Budgets → Set a budget**, choose the category, type the
   **Monthly limit**, and leave **Every month** on. Tap **Save**.
   - If you've chosen a second currency in Settings, two buttons (for example **UAH** and **EUR**)
     appear above the limit. Pick **EUR** to set the limit in euros — handy for travel or anything
     you pay for in that currency.
2. Open **Home**. Under the month's spending you'll see a **Budgets** card.
3. Tap **All budgets** to see every limit, change one, or look at another month with the ‹ › arrows.

## What success looks like

- The **Budgets** card lists only the categories that need a look: 80% or more of the limit used,
  already over it, or spending fast enough to go over by the end of the month ("On track for …").
- Each line shows spent / limit and a bar. A red bar and "… over" mean the limit is passed.
- When every limit is fine, the card just says "All 3 budgets on track".
- A limit set in euros is shown in euros ("€210 / €300"), with what it's worth in your main currency
  today underneath.

## If it doesn't work

- **No Budgets card on Home** — no budget is set yet. Add one in Settings → Budgets.
- **A category is missing** — limits are for expense categories only, and a one-month limit (with
  **Every month** off) only shows in the month it was set for.
- **A euro budget disappeared** — the app has no exchange rate for that currency yet. It comes back
  once rates update (usually overnight).
- **The figure looks too high** — spending in other currencies is counted at the rate on the day it
  was recorded, in your main currency.
