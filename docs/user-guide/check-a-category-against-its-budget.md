# Check a category against its budget

## What this solves

You set a monthly limit for some categories — say Groceries ₴50 000 — and want to see, in your
reports, how much you actually spent against it.

## Steps

1. Set a limit once: **Settings → Budgets → Set a budget**, choose the category, type the
   **Monthly limit** and tap **Save**. The Budgets page is just the list of your limits; tap one to
   change or delete it.
   - If you've chosen a second currency in **Settings → Second currency**, buttons such as
     **UAH** and **EUR** appear above the limit. Pick **EUR** to set the limit in euros.
2. Open **Reports → This month**. Each category with a budget shows "of ₴50 000" under what you
   spent. Tap a bar under the chart to look at another month.
3. In **Reports → By category** (the table), the budget is shown under each category's name.

## What success looks like

- Under the category's amount you see its limit, for example "of ₴50 000".
- A limit set in euros is read in euros: "€763 of €650" — what you spent, converted at today's
  rate, against the limit. In the table it reads "Budget €650 ≈ ₴32 657", next to the hryvnia
  figures.
- An amount in red means that month went over the limit — in the list and in the table.

## If it doesn't work

- **No "of …" under a category** — it has no budget, or it's an income category (budgets are for
  expenses only).
- **A euro budget doesn't show** — the app has no exchange rate for that currency yet. It appears
  once rates update, usually overnight.
- **No budget under the name in the table** — it only shows for **Expenses** by **Months**.
